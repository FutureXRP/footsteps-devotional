# Spiritual Formation — 365 Daily Topics (master list)

The backbone of the series: one formation truth per day, each anchored to a
passage, grouped into 13 stages that move as a journey — from the first
awakening of the restless heart, through surrender, the love of the Father, the
disciplines, the interior depths, the enemy's resistance, the dark night, the
death of self, the fruit of the Spirit, life together, the active life, and at
last the Celestial City.

**This is a draft for curation** — skim, cut, add, reorder. Each line will be
expanded into a full entry in the four-part frame plus the Formation lens (see
`docs/spiritual-formation-authoring.md`), pinned to an exact verse quoted in the
public-domain WEB translation, with a Voice drawn from Scripture or the
public-domain spiritual classics, then run through the verification pass.

Counts: 25 · 30 · 30 · 35 · 25 · 30 · 30 · 30 · 30 · 30 · 25 · 20 · 25 = **365**.

Primary sources by stage: Augustine & Bunyan (1); Chambers, *My Utmost for His
Highest* (2); Brother Lawrence & the Father-heart of God (3); the classic
disciplines (4); the *Sacred Pathways* framework, paraphrased (5); Teresa of
Avila, *Interior Castle* / *Mansions of the Heart* (6); *The Screwtape Letters*,
referenced (7); John of the Cross & Bunyan's valley (8); Thomas a Kempis &
Andrew Murray (9); the fruit of the Spirit (10); the body of Christ (11);
vocation & mercy (12); Bunyan's Celestial City (13). And ultimately Scripture.
