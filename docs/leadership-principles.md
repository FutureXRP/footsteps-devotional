# Biblical Leadership — 365 Principles (master list)

The backbone of the series: one leadership principle per day, each anchored to a
passage, grouped into 13 themes. **This is a draft for curation** — skim, cut,
add, reorder. Passages are chosen to genuinely teach the principle; each will be
pinned to an exact verse and quoted in the public-domain WEB translation, and
run through the verification pass (see `docs/leadership-authoring.md`) when its
full entry is written.

Counts: 30 · 35 · 30 · 35 · 25 · 30 · 35 · 25 · 25 · 30 · 25 · 20 · 20 = **365**.
