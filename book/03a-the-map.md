---

**Four things this diagram is lying about, slightly.**

It is drawn as a straight line and it is not one. You will be reoriented in one
part of your life while still bleeding from a break in another. You will reach the
fourth box and be back in the second by Thursday.

It is drawn with even boxes and they are not even. Phase two is longer than the
other three together, in most lives.

It is drawn with an arrow out of the last box and for some people, in this life,
there isn't one. Prolonged disorientation is a real Christian life, lived by
faithful people, and it is not a failure to progress.

And it is drawn as though someone were reading it from outside. You will be inside
it, where none of the boxes have labels.

*Its one honest claim is the bottom one. He is in all four.*
