# Getting to 50,000 — what it would cost and what it would buy

Current: **25,900**. The question is whether +24,000 helps or hurts.

The answer depends entirely on which of two things you mean by "longer."

---

## The distinction that decides it

**Dilation** — writing the existing scenes at greater length. More interiority,
more description, more sentences per beat. This would destroy the book. The
compression *is* the voice; every reader who has looked at it has identified the
restraint as the primary virtue, and the moment a scene starts explaining itself
the nonfiction writer is back in the room. There is no version of this where
25,900 words of these scenes become 50,000 words of these scenes and the book
survives.

**Addition** — scenes that do not currently exist, in places where the story is
genuinely elided. This is not padding. Some of it would make the book
substantially better, and the evidence is in the density table.

---

## Where the book is actually thin

Words per year of story time:

| Section | Words | Story time | Words / year |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Waste (Grieve) | 7,575 | 2 yr | **3,790** |
| Return | 3,245 | ~0.6 yr | ~5,400 |
| Slow Country | 8,140 | ~1.5 yr | 5,430 |
| Sundering | 2,835 | ~0.6 yr | ~4,700 |
| **The valley** | **4,147** | **7 yr** | **590** |

The valley is **six times more compressed than anything else in the book** — and
it is the section two independent readers named as the part that makes the book
matter. Seven years, forty-one people, a nine-year engineering project, a death
that never happens on the page, and a two-year argument about fire, all in 4,100
words. That is not restraint. That is a montage standing in for a movement.

That is where the words are.

---

## What I would actually add — about +15,500

### The valley: +6,000 (590 → ~1,450 words/year)

- **Rell gets one human irregularity.** She cannot read. The woman who holds nine
  years of road in her head, who knows the fall of every culvert, cannot read a
  word — and Ivo reads to her in the workshop, which is what those afternoons
  actually are. One scene establishes it; it is never made a lesson of.
- **Ivo, two or three times earlier**, so that Grieve's *I am not Ivo* lands on a
  man the reader knows rather than a name.
- **Petch as a boy at the edge of things**, twice, before Forty-Nine — so the
  confrontation is not his entrance.
- **The fire project as a lived two years**: the season it stalled, the household
  that pulled out and why, the argument about the cache that Rell won.
- **A death.** Seven years and nobody dies. The old woman who laid the four
  hundred bad yards should die, and the valley should mark it on the road, and
  nobody should say what that means.
- **One more monthly table**, mid-book, where something goes wrong at it.

### Constant on the road: +3,000

Four months of the book's warmest relationship currently exist as montage. Three
or four real scenes — and **the disagreement about something other than the road**
that two readers have now asked for twice. Something concrete: a person they pass
and do not help, or do.

### Grieve's other travellers: +3,000

She had nine. We see two. Add two more inside the two years — including **one she
gets wrong**, which is the only way the reader learns before Steadfast does that
there was never a method. And her own arrival, told once, briefly, in her
register.

### The Return: +2,000

Eleven weeks, five scenes. More Aldo. And **a traveller arriving while he is
there**, so he watches her do it now, from inside the house rather than from the
fold.

### The Slow Country crossing: +1,500

One or two encounters in the long alone stretch, which is currently the least
populated country in the book.

**Running total: ~41,400.** Careful texture in the Waste and the Return takes it
to about **44,000**.

---

## The last 6,000 — and the one honest way to get them

I cannot justify another 6,000 by addition to existing sections. Everything past
~44,000 would be dilation, and dilation is fatal here.

There is exactly one legitimate route to a true 50,000: **write the other fire.**

The man and two women forty miles north currently exist as a report. Steadfast
could go — on the way back from Grieve, or between the valley and the garden — and
it would be a genuine new movement, roughly 5,000 words, earned by everything
before it.

The condition is that it must not resolve anything. They should be nothing like
Grieve. They should have made a different and possibly worse arrangement. They
should not want to be part of anything, and should be faintly suspicious of a man
arriving to tell them about other fires. He should leave without having connected
anything — because the last line of the book is that *none of them knew about each
other yet*, and that has to stay true.

Done that way it deepens the book. Done as a triumphant linking-up it destroys the
ending.

---

## What must not be touched at any length

- **The Sundering.** Its account-voice compression is the point.
- **The four months in the Waste.** *You still say it in months* contains them.
- **The 104-day return journey.** *He will not be interesting about the journey.*
- **Any interiority, anywhere.** Every added word must be event, object, or
  dialogue.

## The risk, stated plainly

Expansion is the single most dangerous thing that could be done to this manuscript,
because it invites back exactly what the drafting discipline kept out. Every new
scene must pass the three constraints unchanged: no prophetic retrospection, nobody
realises they are speaking in aphorism, and the scene must generate the next scene
rather than the next meaning. A scene added to reach a word count will announce
itself.

## Recommendation

**Go to 42–45,000, not 50,000.**

At 26,000 it is a novella, which is genuinely hard to price and shelve. At 42,000
it is a short novel and the commercial objection disappears. The 15,500 words above
would make it a better book — particularly the valley, which is currently the
weakest execution of the strongest idea.

The last 5–8,000 to hit a round 50,000 would be the first words in this project
written for a number rather than for the story, and it would show.

If 50,000 is a hard requirement, write the other fire — and write it as a failure
to connect.
